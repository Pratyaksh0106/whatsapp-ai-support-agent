# Payments and cancellation

## Payment methods
We accept UPI, debit and credit cards, net banking and cash on delivery (COD).

## Cash on delivery
COD is available on orders up to ₹5,000 with a ₹49 handling fee. Orders above ₹5,000 must be prepaid.

## Cancellation
Orders can be cancelled free of charge until they are shipped. Once an order is shipped it cannot be cancelled, but you can refuse delivery or return it within 30 days.
