# Shipping

## Delivery times
Standard shipping takes 3-5 business days across India. Express shipping takes 1-2 business days and costs ₹149. Remote pin codes may take up to 3 extra days.

## Shipping charges
Standard shipping is free on orders above ₹999. Below ₹999 the standard shipping fee is ₹60.

## Tracking
Once an order is shipped you receive a tracking id by WhatsApp. Tracking updates can take up to 12 hours to appear.
