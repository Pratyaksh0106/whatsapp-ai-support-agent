# Returns and refunds

## Return window
You can return any unused product in original packaging within 30 days of delivery. Opened earphones can only be returned if they are defective.

## How refunds work
Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5-7 business days after we receive and inspect the returned item. For cash on delivery orders, refunds are sent by bank transfer after you share your account details with support.

## Damaged or wrong items
If your item arrives damaged, defective or incorrect, report it within 7 days of delivery with a photo. We arrange a free pickup and send a replacement or refund.
